Nuestra Historia

Our story

For over ten years, Rio Alto has brought the heart of Mexico City and its nearby provinces to High River. Every dish is crafted in our traditional Mexican oven — the kind you won’t find anywhere else in Alberta.

Alberta beef, vegetables from farms down the road, sauces built from raw ingredients, sweet bread from the morning bake — nothing from a can, ever.

Our guests say dining here is like taking a trip to Mexico without leaving Alberta. Come taste why.

— Héctor López founder & cook
